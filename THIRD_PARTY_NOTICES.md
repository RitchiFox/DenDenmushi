## BlackHole

The separate virtual microphone and speaker drivers are built from
[ExistentialAudio/BlackHole](https://github.com/ExistentialAudio/BlackHole),
commit `ffcb74433fbcf8c8ca5c736677c1a4864384dc09`.
Complete vendored source and upstream notices are in `vendor/BlackHole/`.
See `vendor/BlackHole/LICENSE` and individual source headers for licensing.
`scripts/build-wifi-drivers.py` changes build-time names, identifiers, channel
count and sample rates; it retains the upstream C implementation.

The generated installer includes the upstream license. If you redistribute
compiled drivers, also supply the corresponding source and build scripts
under the upstream license. The driver installer is built locally, not
committed as an opaque binary.

## Artwork and sound

The two eye illustrations in `Resources/eye-*-law.png` were generated with
OpenAI image generation for this project. Prompts and reference description
are retained in `Resources/eye-art-prompts.txt`. They are fan illustrations
inspired by the Den Den Mushi associated with Trafalgar Law in One Piece.
No ownership of One Piece characters or trademarks is claimed and this
project is not affiliated with their owners. The GPL grant does not grant
rights to third-party characters or trademarks.

`pi/waiting.wav` is an original synthesized two-tone cue generated by
`scripts/generate-waiting-tone.py`; no YouTube recording is included.
